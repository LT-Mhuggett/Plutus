## Missing capability

| # | Finding | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **F** | ⚠ **Split payments are not offered.** "I need this on the webtill too. The functionality needs to be the same." | `Client.Core/TenderLoop` ALREADY implements split tendering and is tested for it (19 tests, including "the surcharge is applied ONCE across a split card payment"). The rule exists; the SCREEN never offers a second tender. ⚠ Both tills — Part B row, not a MAUI-only fix |
| **G** | ⚠ **Refunds must offer ONLY the tender the sale was paid with.** "If it was a card payment, needs to go back to card." | Refunding a card sale in cash is how a shop is used to launder a card. The original tender is on the stored payload |
| **H** | **Stock write-offs are not reported anywhere.** "Where is this captured? I need a report on the portal (that will then be reflected in all tills) that shows stock adjustments" | The LEDGER already records every movement with its reason and actor (`StockMovement`). What is missing is a portal REPORT over it |
| **I** | **Z-close variance is not flagged.** Open £150, spend £20, close £110 → should be flagged, and there should be a portal report/warning for a till that closed short | ⚠ The server already computes expected-vs-counted; nothing surfaces the variance |
